Our story

A small table by the harbour.

Kandu means “sea channel” in Dhivehi. We cook the way island kitchens always have: what the sea and the season give us, with chilli, curry leaf, smoked tuna and lime. Mornings are for roshi and mas huni, evenings for the grill.
